Privacy Policy

Insert Laughs · package app.insertlaughs.play · last updated 18 September 2026

Insert Laughs is a fill-in-the-blank storytelling app. There is no account, no ads, and no analytics server that receives your stories.

What stays on the device

  • Built-in story templates that ship with the app
  • Custom templates and filled-in words you create or save, until you delete them or uninstall the app

Sharing

If you choose Share, Android’s share sheet sends that story or template to the app or contact you pick (Messages, email, etc.). Insert Laughs does not upload shared content to our own servers.

Deep links

If someone sends you an Insert Laughs link and you open it with the app installed, the app may import that template onto this device. That happens only when you open the link.

Permissions

The app only uses permissions needed for normal Android sharing and deep links. It does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or location for core play.

Children

Insert Laughs is intended for a general audience and has no social feed or accounts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: the Play Store listing contact for Insert Laughs, or the developer email on the store page.
