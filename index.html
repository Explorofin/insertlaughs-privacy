Insert Laughs Privacy Policy

Insert Laughs is a fill-in-the-blank story game. It does not create accounts, show ads, or send your words to our servers — because we do not run one.

Effective September 18, 2026 · Last updated September 18, 2026

Short version. Stories you type stay on your phone. Sharing uses Android’s share sheet and goes only where you send it. Insert Laughs does not collect analytics, show ads, sell data, or require an account.

Who publishes Insert Laughs

Insert Laughs (Android package app.insertlaughs.play) is published by Ryan Seng. This policy covers the Play Store build of that app.

What the app does

You browse built-in stories, fill in blanks, read the finished story, and optionally share it. You can also write custom templates, save them on the device, play them, and send a template link to someone else who has Insert Laughs.

Information we do not collect

Insert Laughs does not include an account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting backend, or our own cloud API. The app does not request the Internet permission. We do not collect, sell, or share:

  • Names, emails, or login credentials (there is nothing to sign in to)
  • Advertising IDs or tracking profiles
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • Usage metrics sent to us

What stays on your device

Custom stories you create or import are stored only in the app’s private local files (a JSON file in the app sandbox). Built-in stories ship inside the app. Fill-in answers live in memory while you play and are not uploaded.

If Android Backup / device-to-device transfer is enabled on your phone, those local files may be included in the backup the operating system already makes. That copy is controlled by Google / your device maker, not by an Insert Laughs server — there is none.

Uninstalling Insert Laughs deletes the app’s local custom stories.

Sharing (you choose the destination)

When you tap share, Insert Laughs hands plain text to the Android share sheet (ACTION_SEND). You pick Messages, email, or another app. That text may be:

  • A completed story — readable by anyone, no app required
  • A custom template plus an insertlaughs:// or HTTPS deep-link payload so another Insert Laughs install can open the blanks

We do not receive a copy of what you share. The destination app’s own privacy policy applies once you send it (for example, your carrier or messaging app).

If someone shares a template into Insert Laughs, the app reads that text only to import the story you chose to open. It is not forwarded onward unless you share again.

Permissions

Insert Laughs does not declare Internet, location, contacts, storage, or camera permissions. Sharing and opening links use standard Android intents you trigger.

Children

Insert Laughs is a word game intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — we do not operate a collection pipeline. Parents should know that anything typed into blanks and then shared will be visible to the people or apps they share with. Keep shared stories Play-Store-friendly.

Third parties

Google Play distributes the app. Android may apply backup, Play Protect, and OS-level diagnostics under Google’s policies. Insert Laughs does not embed additional third-party analytics, ads, or social SDKs.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way (for example, if a future version added a network service), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. The version linked from the Play listing is the current one.
